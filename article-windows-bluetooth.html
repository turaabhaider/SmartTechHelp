Connect Bluetooth Devices on Windows

Want to connect a Bluetooth device to your Windows PC? Follow these steps to pair and troubleshoot your devices easily.

Step 1: Turn On Bluetooth

Go to Settings → Devices → Bluetooth & Other Devices → Turn on Bluetooth.

Step 2: Pair Device

Click “Add Bluetooth or other device” → Select Bluetooth → Choose your device from the list → Pair.

Step 3: Troubleshoot Connection

If the device doesn’t connect, restart Bluetooth, update drivers, or remove and re-add the device.

Step 4: Update Bluetooth Drivers

Go to Device Manager → Bluetooth → Update driver. This fixes most pairing issues.
