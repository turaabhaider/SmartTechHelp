How to Recover Deleted WhatsApp Messages

Accidentally deleted important WhatsApp messages? This guide will show you how to recover them on Android and iPhone.

Method 1: Google Drive Backup (Android)

Uninstall WhatsApp → Reinstall → Verify number → Tap Restore to retrieve messages from backup.

Method 2: Local Backup (Android)

Navigate to /WhatsApp/Databases/ → Rename latest backup → Reinstall WhatsApp → Restore from local backup.

Method 3: iCloud Backup (iPhone)

Ensure iCloud backup is current → Delete WhatsApp → Reinstall → Restore Chat History from iCloud.

Method 4: Third-Party Tools

Trusted tools like Dr.Fone or PhoneRescue can recover messages if backup methods fail.

Tips

  • Back up regularly to Google Drive or iCloud.
  • Act quickly to prevent older messages from being overwritten.
  • Use only trusted third-party recovery apps.
