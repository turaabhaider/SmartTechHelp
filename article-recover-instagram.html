How to Recover Instagram Account

Lost access to your Instagram account? Follow these methods to regain access safely.

Method 1: Reset Password

Open Instagram → Tap “Forgot Password?” → Enter email, username, or phone number → Follow instructions.

Method 2: Verify Your Identity

If hacked, follow Instagram’s identity verification process to secure your account.

Method 3: Contact Instagram Support

Use the “Need more help?” option in the app to report your issue and recover your account.

Tips

  • Use a strong password and enable 2FA.
  • Do not share your account credentials with others.
