How to Free Up Storage on Android

Low storage on Android can slow down your device and prevent app installations. Follow these steps to free up space safely.

Step 1: Clear App Cache

Settings → Storage → Cached data → Clear Cache.

Step 2: Delete Unused Apps

Uninstall apps you no longer use to free space.

Step 3: Move Media to SD Card or Cloud

Transfer photos, videos, and documents to an SD card or Google Photos/Drive.

Step 4: Use Storage Management Tools

Android built-in storage tools or apps like Files by Google can help clean junk files.

Step 5: Clear Downloads Folder

Delete old files in the Downloads folder that are no longer needed.
