Recover Deleted WhatsApp Messages on Android

Accidentally deleted WhatsApp messages on Android? Follow this guide to recover them easily using Google Drive backups, local backups, or third-party tools.

Method 1: Google Drive Backup

Uninstall WhatsApp → Reinstall → Verify number → Tap “Restore” when prompted to restore messages from Google Drive.

Method 2: Local Backup

Open /WhatsApp/Databases/ on your device → Rename the latest backup → Reinstall WhatsApp → Restore from local backup.

Method 3: Third-Party Tools

Use trusted apps like Dr.Fone or PhoneRescue to recover messages if backup methods fail.

Tips

  • Back up WhatsApp regularly to Google Drive.
  • Act quickly before old messages get overwritten.
  • Use trusted third-party tools only.
