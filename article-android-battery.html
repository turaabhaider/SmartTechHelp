Tips to Improve Android Battery Life

Running out of battery quickly? Follow these practical tips to extend your Android phone’s battery life significantly.

1. Reduce Screen Brightness

Lower your screen brightness or use adaptive brightness to save battery.

2. Turn Off Unused Features

Disable Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and GPS when not in use.

3. Use Battery Saver Mode

Settings → Battery → Battery Saver to limit background activities.

4. Manage Apps Running in Background

Close unnecessary apps and restrict background data for heavy apps.

5. Update Apps and OS

Keep apps and system updated to benefit from battery optimizations.
