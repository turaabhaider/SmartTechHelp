Fix Play Store Download Pending

If your Play Store apps are stuck on “Download Pending”, follow these steps to resolve the issue.

Step 1: Check Internet Connection

Make sure you are connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data with good speed.

Step 2: Clear Cache and Data

Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear Cache and Clear Data.

Step 3: Restart Device

Sometimes a simple restart fixes the download pending problem.

Step 4: Update Play Store

Ensure Play Store is up to date from Settings → Apps → Google Play Store.

Step 5: Remove Google Account

Settings → Accounts → Remove and re-add your Google account if needed.
