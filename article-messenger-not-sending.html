Fix Messenger Not Sending Messages

Messenger failing to send messages? These troubleshooting steps will help fix the issue.

Step 1: Check Internet Connection

Ensure stable Wi-Fi or mobile data connection.

Step 2: Restart Messenger

Close the app completely and reopen it.

Step 3: Clear Cache and Data

Settings → Apps → Messenger → Storage → Clear Cache and Clear Data.

Step 4: Update Messenger

Go to Play Store or App Store and update the Messenger app.

Step 5: Reinstall Messenger

Uninstall and reinstall the app to fix persistent issues.
